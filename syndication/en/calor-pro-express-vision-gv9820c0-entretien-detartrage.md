## 🚨 Error Codes & Routine Maintenance Lights

### ⚠️ Voyant Calc/Clean (orange clignotant)
- **Common Cause:** Routine descaling reminder triggered
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Fill water tank with descaling solution and water, run automatic cycle, then flush thoroughly.

### ⚠️ Voyant 'Réservoir vide'
- **Common Cause:** Clean water tank empty or dirty container full
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Empty dirty container, clean float sensor and refill clean water tank.

### ⚠️ Voyant 'Pas de vapeur' (fixe)
- **Common Cause:** Alert code Voyant 'Pas de vapeur' (fixe): routine sensor check required
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Turn off power for 30s, wipe external optical contacts and restart machine.
