## 🚨 Error Codes & Routine Maintenance Lights

### ⚠️ Voyant "Check Brush-Roll"
- **Common Cause:** Roller brush stalled or tangled by hair
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Release quick-clip cover, remove roller and cut away tangled hairs with scissors.

### ⚠️ Voyant "Clean Filter"
- **Common Cause:** Filter clogged or airflow obstructed
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Remove and tap out dust or wash filter under cold water. Let dry for 24h before reinserting.

### ⚠️ Voyant "Max Fill Line" sur le bac à poussière
- **Common Cause:** Alert code Voyant "Max Fill Line" sur le bac à poussière: routine sensor check required
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Turn off power for 30s, wipe external optical contacts and restart machine.
