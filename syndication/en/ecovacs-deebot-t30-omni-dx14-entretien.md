## 🚨 Error Codes & Routine Maintenance Lights

### ⚠️ Brosse principale emmêlée
- **Common Cause:** Roller brush stalled or tangled by hair
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Release quick-clip cover, remove roller and cut away tangled hairs with scissors.

### ⚠️ Réservoir d'eau sale plein
- **Common Cause:** Clean water tank empty or dirty container full
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Empty dirty container, clean float sensor and refill clean water tank.

### ⚠️ Capteur de chute sale
- **Common Cause:** Alert code Capteur de chute sale: routine sensor check required
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Turn off power for 30s, wipe external optical contacts and restart machine.
