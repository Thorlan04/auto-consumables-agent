## 🚨 Error Codes & Routine Maintenance Lights

### ⚠️ Voyant de remplacement du filtre (rouge/clignotant)
- **Common Cause:** Filter clogged or airflow obstructed
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Remove and tap out dust or wash filter under cold water. Let dry for 24h before reinserting.

### ⚠️ Voyant Wi-Fi éteint
- **Common Cause:** Clean water tank empty or dirty container full
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Empty dirty container, clean float sensor and refill clean water tank.

### ⚠️ Voyant de qualité de l'air (bleu/vert/orange/rouge) ne change pas
- **Common Cause:** Alert code Voyant de qualité de l'air (bleu/vert/orange/rouge) ne change pas: routine sensor check required
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Turn off power for 30s, wipe external optical contacts and restart machine.
