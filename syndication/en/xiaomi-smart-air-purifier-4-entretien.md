## 🚨 Error Codes & Routine Maintenance Lights

### ⚠️ Voyant filtre clignotant / Message 'Replace Filter'
- **Common Cause:** Filter clogged or airflow obstructed
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Remove and tap out dust or wash filter under cold water. Let dry for 24h before reinserting.

### ⚠️ Affichage PM2.5 incohérent ou élevé après nettoyage
- **Common Cause:** Alert code Affichage PM2.5 incohérent ou élevé après nettoyage: routine sensor check required
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Turn off power for 30s, wipe external optical contacts and restart machine.

### ⚠️ Ventilation faible ou bruit excessif
- **Common Cause:** Alert code Ventilation faible ou bruit excessif: routine sensor check required
- **Tool-Free Fix:** Turn off power for 30s, wipe external optical contacts and restart machine.
